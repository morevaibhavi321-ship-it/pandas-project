# Customer's Shopping Behavior

## Project Overview
This project is about what customer buys the products throughout the seasons.how often customer purchases stuffs.customer's rating for products.

## Dataset
[customer_shopping_behavior.csv](/Data/shopping_trends_updated.csv)

## Tools
- Pandas

## Analysis Performed

- Number of customers based on age groups
- Top 10 most purchased products
- Purchase amount by category
- Subscribed and non subscribed puchase amount
- Payment method 
- groups by location and category based on total purchase
- Customer segment and their revenue
- categories with high purchase volume and low average purchase amount
- item purchased with strong average rating and low purchased volume
